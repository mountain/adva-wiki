# Process residue

A **process residue** is the retained residual of a process — the part of the
process data that is not collapsed by a quotient, an observer, or a [[transport]],
and that must be kept rather than erased.  The complete process residual is the
unchanged exact process slice, and it prevents an operation from becoming a
purely extensional (value-only) equivalence.

A simple endpoint law may carry an already nontrivial process residual: the
residual is the process-level datum that survives the value.  The process
residue is part of the [[retained history]] and is distinct from the
[[process history]] it records.

# 过程残差

**过程残差**是过程被保留的残差——未被商、观察者或传输坍缩、且必须保留而非抹除的那部分过程数据。
完整过程残差是未改变的精确过程切片，它阻止运算沦为纯外延（仅值）等价。

简单的端点律可能携带已非平凡的过程残差：残差是存活于值之上的过程级数据。过程残差是保留历史的
一部分，区别于它所记录的过程历史。
