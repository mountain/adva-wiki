# State transport

**State transport** is the forward [[transport]] of a process's state — the state
motion under the process — dual to the backward [[predicate transport]]
$f^*(P)=f^{-1}(P)$.  State evaluation and the truth of transported predicates
are two readings of one [[state observer duality]].

A transported state is carried forward with the process; [[sheet transport]] is
the instance on a cover where the state mark moves between sheets.  State
transport is the forward side of transport, paired against the
[[backward transport]] of observers and predicates.

# 状态传输

**状态传输**是过程状态的前向传输——过程下的状态运动——与后向谓词传输 $f^*(P)=f^{-1}(P)$ 对偶。
状态求值与所传输谓词的真值是状态-观察者对偶的两种读数。

被传输状态随过程前向携带；叶面传输是覆盖上状态标记在叶面间移动的实例。状态传输是传输的前向
一侧，与观察者与谓词的后向传输相配。
