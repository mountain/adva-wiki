# Arithmetic flow equation

The **arithmetic flow equation** governs the propagation of the [[assignment]]
along [[arithmetic path|arithmetic paths]]: $\frac{da}{ds}=\mu\cos\theta+a\lambda\sin\theta$, where
$\theta$ is the angle of movement.  Its coordinate-free form is the
[[eikonal equation]] $|\nabla a|^2=\mu^2+\lambda^2a^2$, equivalently a
Hamilton–Jacobi equation, and the assignment $a=-x/y$ satisfies it.

This is the legacy terminology of the earlier manuscripts; in Paper 0 the
[[eikonal]] identity is the regular-AES axiom, and the [[continuous arithmetic motion]]
is its infinitesimal counterpart.

# 算术流方程

**算术流方程**支配赋值沿算术路径的传播：$\frac{da}{ds}=\mu\cos\theta+a\lambda\sin\theta$，其中
$\theta$ 是运动角度。其坐标无关形式是程函方程 $|\nabla a|^2=\mu^2+\lambda^2a^2$（等价于
Hamilton–Jacobi 方程），赋值 $a=-x/y$ 满足它。

这是早期手稿的遗留术语；在 Paper 0 中程函恒等式是正则 AES 公理，连续算术运动是其无穷小对应。
