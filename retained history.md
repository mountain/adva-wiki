# Retained history

**Retained history** is the ordered process/[[occurrence history]] that a carrier,
frame, or presentation keeps as a first-class output rather than discarding.  In
the Adva toolchain a frame always retains the `history/result/evidence` output
ports: the history is kept alongside the result and the evidence, and a recorded
triple is not treated as proof that execution produced it.

Retention is an obligation, not an optimization: passing to a quotient, a state,
or a value must not silently erase the history, and a byte-projection or
normalization judgment carries no permission to erase retained history.  A
presentation must record the transformation, the selected checked program and
its typed boundary, source/[[occurrence transport]], and the retained history.

The term contrasts with the [[process history]] that is deliberately forgotten: the
retained history is the part that survives, and it is what makes a carrier state
richer than its endpoint value or its commutative summary.

# 保留历史

**保留历史**是载体、框架或展示作为一等输出而保留的有序过程/出现历史，而非被丢弃。在 Adva
工具链中，框架总是保留 `history/result/evidence` 三个输出端口：历史与结果、证据一起保留，
而记录的三元组不被当作执行产生它的证明。

保留是一项义务，而非优化：商、状态或值化不得悄悄抹去历史，字节投影或归一化判断也不携带抹除
保留历史的许可。展示必须记录变换、选定的经检验程序及其类型边界、源/出现传输，以及保留历史。

该词与故意被遗忘的过程历史相对：保留历史是存留下来的那一部分，它使载体状态比其端点值或交换
摘要更丰富。
