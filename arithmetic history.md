# Arithmetic history

An **arithmetic history** is a finite chronological word
$\gamma=(g_1,\ldots,g_n)$ of elementary arithmetic steps, each either an
additive step $x\mapsto x+p$ or a multiplicative step $x\mapsto e^q x$.  It is
the add–scale history of the [[history group]] $G$, applied in order, and it
evaluates to an [[affine motion]] via $\nu:G\to\operatorname{Aff}^+(1,\mathbb R)$.

The arithmetic history is the chronological object to which the charges, the
[[charge path]], and the [[ACS]] are attached; two histories may induce the same affine
motion while carrying different histories.  It is the arithmetic-sector
instance of the [[process history]].

# 算术历史

**算术历史**是初等算术步的有限时间顺序词 $\gamma=(g_1,\ldots,g_n)$，每步是加法步
$x\mapsto x+p$ 或乘法步 $x\mapsto e^q x$。它是历史群 $G$ 的加减历史，按顺序应用，经
$\nu:G\to\operatorname{Aff}^+(1,\mathbb R)$ 求值为仿射运动。

算术历史是荷、荷路径与 ACS 所依附的时间顺序对象；两条历史可诱导同一仿射运动却携带不同历史。
它是过程历史的算术扇形实例。
