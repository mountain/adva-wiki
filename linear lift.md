# Linear lift

A **linear lift** is a choice of a linear (matrix) representative of a
projective transformation, lifting from $\operatorname{PGL}_2$ to
$\operatorname{SL}_2$ (or $\operatorname{GL}_2$); it retains the central sign
that the [[projective matrix]] class forgets.  The matrix $-I$ is a central
choice, and the central sign belongs to the choice of linear lift.

For example the reciprocal switch $J:z\mapsto-1/z$ has $J^2=-I$ in its linear
lift and projective square $1$: it is order four in the lift and order two
projectively.  A constant two-dimensional linear lift projectivizes to at most a
quadratic scalar field, so a generic cubic term cannot arise from the same
mechanism.

# 线性提升

**线性提升**是射影变换的线性（矩阵）代表的一个选择，从 $\operatorname{PGL}_2$ 提升到
$\operatorname{SL}_2$（或 $\operatorname{GL}_2$）；它保留射影矩阵类所遗忘的中心符号。矩阵 $-I$
是中心选择，中心符号属于线性提升的选择。

例如倒数开关 $J:z\mapsto-1/z$ 在其线性提升中有 $J^2=-I$、射影平方为 $1$：在提升中是四阶、射影
中为二阶。常数二维线性提升射影化后至多是二次标量场，故一般三次项不能来自同一机制。
