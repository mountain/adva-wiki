# New rank

A **new rank** is a rank opened by objectification: a stable lower-rank process
is objectified into a primitive, which generates a [[higher rank]] free
composition language — a new [[compositional rank]].  The arithmetic and process
readings are the [[new arithmetic rank]] and the [[new process rank]].

A new rank must admit [[compositional rank lowering]] back to the lower-rank
semantics; without that lowering, the proposed primitive does not open a genuine
new rank.

# 新秩

**新秩**是对象化所开辟的秩：稳定的低秩过程被对象化为原始，生成更高秩的自由复合语言——新的复合秩。
算术与过程读数分别是新算术秩与新过程秩。

新秩必须容许复合秩下降到低秩语义；没有该下降，提议原始就不能开辟真正的新秩。
