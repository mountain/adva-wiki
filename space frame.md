# Space frame

A **space frame** is the spatial bracket `[a b c]` of the iota dual machine: an
ordered family of simultaneously present subterms with no head, where no element
decides any other.  It is the space coordinate of the dual stack, paired against
the [[time frame]] `(a b)`, whose head decides the rest.

There is no [[reduction rule]] whose head is a space frame: `[]` orders and collects
and never rewrites, so a space frame is a value, and its evaluation is the
evaluation of its components.  The claim that `()` is time and `[]` is space is a
bounded hypothesis about two nested brackets, asserted only for `()` and `[]`;
the time regime in general is not yet reproduced.

# 空间框架

**空间框架**是 iota 双机器的空间括号 `[a b c]`：同时在场、无头的有序子项族，没有元素决定任何其它
元素。它是双栈的空间坐标，与时间框架 `(a b)`（其头决定其余）相对。

没有头为空间框架的归约规则：`[]` 排序并收集、从不重写，故空间框架是值，其求值即其各分量的求值。
「`()` 是时间、`[]` 是空间」是对两个嵌套括号的有界假设，仅对 `()` 与 `[]` 断言；一般时间机制尚未
复现。
