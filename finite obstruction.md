# Finite obstruction

A **finite obstruction** is an obstruction established already in the finite
setting: a supplied program, schedule, or endpoint condition fails a finite
check, or a construction cannot recover its full data from finite dyadic cuts.
The answer "no" is shown in general already by a finite counterexample, and a
finite equivalence is proved as a [[local obstruction]] law.

A finite obstruction is stated against a [[finite model]] (often a
[[declared model]]); it shows that the failure is not an artifact of a limiting
or infinite process, but is present at the finite level.

# 有限阻碍

**有限阻碍**是已在有限设定中确立的阻碍：所供程序、调度或端点条件未通过有限检查，或构造无法从
有限二进切割恢复其完整数据。答案「否」一般已由有限反例在一般情形中示出，有限等价被证明为局部
阻碍律。

有限阻碍针对有限模型（常为声明模型）陈述；它表明失败不是极限或无限过程的假象，而是已存在于
有限层。
