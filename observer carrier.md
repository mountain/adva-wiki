# Observer carrier

An **observer carrier** is the carrier on which an observer's data lives — the
space of observations, such as a finite observation carrier or an
exp-polynomial observer carrier in a chart.  It must be incidence-level, and it
is chart- and observer-relative; the exact observer carrier may be split across
a [[middle cut]].

The observer carrier is the backward side of the [[state observer duality]],
paired against the [[state carrier]]; it is transported by [[observer transport]]
and framed by an [[observer frame]].

# 观察者载体

**观察者载体**是观察者数据所居的载体——观察的空间，如图表中的有限观察载体或指数多项式观察者
载体。它必须是关联级的，且是图表与观察者相对的；精确观察者载体可被中间切割分裂。

观察者载体是状态-观察者对偶的后向一侧，与状态载体相配；它由观察者传输所传输、由观察者框架所
框架化。
