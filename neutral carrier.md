# Neutral carrier

A **neutral carrier** is a vertex of the carrier graph that is kept
group-neutral: it does not presuppose a program, proof, or model kind.  It
contains only an artifact cache coordinate and a canonical open frontier — no
mechanism and no persistent semantic kind.

Neutrality constrains vertices, not edges: the carriers stay neutral while the
transitions between them are mechanism-labelled transition frames carrying a
subject/method/object triple and a history/result/evidence output boundary.
Carrier identifiers are document-local storage coordinates, not sources,
occurrences, or values.  A neutral carrier is distinct from a [[declared carrier]],
which declares its directions, and from an [[exact carrier]], which retains full
decoration.

# 中性载体

**中性载体**是保持群中性的载体图顶点：它不预设程序、证明或模型种类。它只包含工件缓存坐标与
规范开前沿——无机制、无持久语义种类。

中性约束顶点而非边：载体保持中性，而它们之间的转移是机制标注的转移框架，携带
subject/method/object 三元与 history/result/evidence 输出边界。载体标识是文档局部存储坐标，非源、
出现或值。中性载体区别于声明方向的声明载体，也区别于保留完整装饰的精确载体。
