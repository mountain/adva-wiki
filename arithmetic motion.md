# Arithmetic motion

An **arithmetic motion** is one of the four operations acting as a motion of
points in the [[basic hyperbolic model]] $E_0$: the [[assignment]] translation
$\mathsf X_s(x,y)=(x-sy,y)$ adds $s$ to the assignment, and the assignment
scaling $\mathsf Y_k(x,y)=(x,y/k)$ multiplies it by $k$; subtraction and
division come from the same two families and a reflection.

The arithmetic motions are assignment-compatible but not generally isometries —
they record arithmetic propagation, not the infinitesimal geometry.  They are
the discrete counterpart of the [[continuous arithmetic motion]], and they
generate the [[e0 grid]]; on the assignment line they evaluate to the
[[affine motion]] $\varphi(z)=\Phi z+\xi$.

# 算术运动

**算术运动**是四个运算作为基本双曲模型 $E_0$ 中点运动的实现：赋值平移
$\mathsf X_s(x,y)=(x-sy,y)$ 把赋值加 $s$，赋值伸缩 $\mathsf Y_k(x,y)=(x,y/k)$ 把赋值乘 $k$；减法
与除法来自同样两族及一反射。

算术运动赋值相容但一般非等距——它们记录算术传播而非无穷小几何。它们是连续算术运动的离散对应，
生成 E0 网格；在赋值线上它们求值为仿射运动 $\varphi(z)=\Phi z+\xi$。
