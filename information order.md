# Information order

The **information order** is the refinement preorder on observers and
presentations: $Q\preceq Q'$ when $Q'$ carries at least as much information as
$Q$, i.e. $Q'$ refines $Q$.  It is the [[refinement order]] under which
entailment and minimality are stated, and a "minimal" presentation must name the
information order under which it is minimal.

The [[observable quotient]] partially orders its presentations by the
information order, distinct from the [[causal order]] and the [[event order]],
which order events rather than information content.

# 信息序

**信息序**是观察者与展示上的细化预序：当 $Q'$ 至少携带与 $Q$ 同样多信息（即 $Q'$ 细化 $Q$）时
$Q\preceq Q'$。它是蕴含与极小性所依据的细化序，「极小」展示必须指明其极小所依据的信息序。

可观测量商按信息序对其展示偏序，区别于对事件而非信息内容排序的因果序与事件序。
