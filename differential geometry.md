# Differential geometry

**Differential geometry** is the discipline of the metric, connection, and
[[curvature]] — the geometric language in which the [[arithmetic expression]] spaces
are described.  A [[riemannian metric]] measures infinitesimal geometry, a
[[differential connection]] differentiates along horizontal directions, and the
[[covariant differential]] is the curvature-sensitive operator induced by the
connection.

In AEG the [[contact model]] is the differential-geometric carrier: its
curvature and horizontal distribution are read in differential-geometric
terms, and differential geometry supplies the level at which the arithmetic
structures become metric and curved.

# 微分几何

**微分几何**是度量、联络与曲率的学科——描述算术表达式空间的几何语言。黎曼度量度量无穷小几何，
微分联络沿水平方向求导，共变微分是联络诱导的曲率敏感算子。

在 AEG 中接触模型是微分几何载体：其曲率与水平分布在微分几何术语中读出，微分几何提供算术结构
变为度量与弯曲的层级。
