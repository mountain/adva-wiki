# State carrier

A **state carrier** is the state space that carries the states of a process — a
finite or continuous carrier on which a step alphabet or action acts, and on
which observers and quotients are declared.  In the bounded case it is a finite
set $S$ with a [[partial action]] alphabet $A$.

The carrier state is richer than a bare diagram: two equal diagrams need not be
the same carrier state.  The state carrier is kept distinct from the
[[retained history]] (the ordered record) and from any quotient that collapses
states; the [[six state carrier]] of the causal-cut calibration is one concrete
finite instance, and the [[exact carrier]] is the identity-rich state carrier that
retains full decoration.

# 状态载体

**状态载体**是承载过程状态的状态空间——一个有限或连续载体，步字母表或作用在其上作用，观察者与
商也在其上声明。在有界情形它是一个有限集 $S$ 及部分作用字母表 $A$。

载体状态比裸图更丰富：两个相等的图不必是同一载体状态。状态载体与保留历史（有序记录）及任何
坍缩状态的商相互区分；因果切割校准的六态载体是一个具体有限实例，精确载体则是保留完整装饰的
身份丰富状态载体。
