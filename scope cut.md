# Scope cut

The **scope cut** is the incidence relation between the forward scope
organization of a program (syntax scopes) and the reverse cut organization
([[causal cut|causal cuts]]).  It is a set-valued map, not a single-valued one:
the graft and slice data do not uniquely determine a scope-faithful,
surgery-stable cut placement, and any single-valued selector adds a policy not
contained in the frame.

No bijection between syntax scopes and causal cuts is proved, and a single-valued
scope-to-cut map requires extra structure.  The honest candidate is the finite
scope/cut incidence relation, read along the [[scope path]].

# 作用域切割

**作用域切割**是程序前向作用域组织（句法作用域）与反向切割组织（因果切割）之间的关联关系。它是
集值映射而非单值映射：graft 与 slice 数据不唯一确定作用域忠实、手术稳定的切割放置，任何单值选择
器都添加了框架所不含的策略。

未证明句法作用域与因果切割之间的双射，单值作用域到切割映射需要额外结构。诚实候选是有限
作用域/切割关联关系，沿作用域路径读出。
