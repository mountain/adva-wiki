# History remain

A **history remain** is the singular instance of what survives an operation —
one residual piece of history that is still attached after a quotient, a
circuit, or a [[transport]].  It is the singular form of [[history remains]].

A history remain is carried as a [[retained history]] fibre, and it is the
process-level datum that the operation must not erase.

# 历史留存

**历史留存**是运算后存活的单数实例——商、回路或传输之后仍附着的一残余历史片段。它是历史留存
（复数）的单数形式。

历史留存作为保留历史纤维被携带，是运算不得抹除的过程级数据。
