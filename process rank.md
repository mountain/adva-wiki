# Process rank

A **process rank** is the vertical abstraction level of a process: semantic
compression objectifies a stable lower-rank process or action into a new
primitive, which then generates a higher-rank process language.  The defining
constraint is [[compositional rank lowering]]: every legal higher-rank
composition must admit a coherent interpretation back into lower-rank process
semantics.

The arithmetic tower is the [[model organism]]: addition, multiplication, and
exponentiation are read as [[arithmetic rank|arithmetic ranks]] (equivalently
[[hyperoperation rank|hyperoperation ranks]]), each rank objectifying the
previous one.  The term is a reserved strong theory word: objectification is
selected relative to process semantics, observers, and tasks, and a stable
fibre or quotient point does not become a [[new rank]] by name alone.

# 过程秩

**过程秩**是过程的纵向抽象层级：语义压缩把一个稳定的低秩过程或作用对象化为新原始，后者进而
生成高秩过程语言。定义性约束是组合式降秩：每个合法的高秩组合必须容许连贯地解释回低秩过程
语义。

算术塔是模式生物：加法、乘法、幂运算被读作算术秩（等价地超运算秩），每一秩对象化上一秩。该
词是保留的强理论词：对象化是相对于过程语义、观察者与任务而选定的，稳定纤维或商点不能仅凭
命名就成为新秩。
