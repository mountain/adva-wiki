# Charge endpoint

The **charge endpoint** of an add–scale history $\gamma$ is the terminal point
$(A_\gamma,M_\gamma)$ of its [[charge path]] $C_\gamma$ in the accumulative
commutative space ([[ACS]]): the image of $\gamma$ under the [[charge map]]
$c:G\to\mathbb R^2$, recording the [[total additive charge]] $A_\gamma$ and the
[[total multiplicative charge]] $M_\gamma$ while forgetting the order in which they
were accumulated.

The endpoint is order-insensitive: two histories of the same [[total charge|total charges]] have
the same charge endpoint yet may induce different [[affine motion|affine motions]].  It records
the [[multiplicative charge]], which descends to the [[motion group]], but it does
not record the [[additive charge]] as a motion invariant.  Consequently no
observable that factors through the charge endpoint can detect [[tearing]].

# 荷端点

加减历史 $\gamma$ 的**荷端点**是其荷路径 $C_\gamma$ 在累积交换空间（ACS）中的终点
$(A_\gamma,M_\gamma)$：即 $\gamma$ 在荷映射 $c:G\to\mathbb R^2$ 下的像，记录总加法荷
$A_\gamma$ 与总乘法荷 $M_\gamma$，同时遗忘它们被累积的次序。

端点对次序不敏感：总荷相同的两条历史有相同的荷端点，却可能诱导不同的仿射运动。它记录乘法荷
（下降到运动群），但不把加法荷作为运动不变量。因此任何经荷端点分解的可观测量都不能检测撕裂。
