# Marked spinal history

A **marked spinal history** is the marked form of a [[spinal history]]: a free
marked spinal history over $K$ is a finite chronological word
$\gamma=(g_1,\ldots,g_n)$ of elementary one-hole contexts
$g_i=C_{\omega_i,c_i}^{(\varepsilon_i)}$ with $\varepsilon_i\in\{1,2\}$, where the
superscript marks the operand slot carrying the evolving value; its chirality
word is $\varepsilon(\gamma)=\varepsilon_1\cdots\varepsilon_n$.  A bounded marked
spinal history is a pair $(x_0;\gamma)$ with an initial accumulator $x_0$.

A marked sequential tree and a bounded marked spinal history determine each
other uniquely, provided operation labels, constants, operand slots, and the
initial mark are retained.  The definition includes mixed chirality and makes
the marking part of the data; earlier drafts called certain pure-chirality
cases threadlike expressions.

# 标记脊历史

**标记脊历史**是脊历史的标记形式：自由标记脊历史是初等单孔语境
$g_i=C_{\omega_i,c_i}^{(\varepsilon_i)}$（$\varepsilon_i\in\{1,2\}$）的有限时间顺序词
$\gamma=(g_1,\ldots,g_n)$，上标标记承载演化值的操作数槽；其手性词为
$\varepsilon(\gamma)=\varepsilon_1\cdots\varepsilon_n$。有界标记脊历史是带初始累加器 $x_0$ 的对
$(x_0;\gamma)$。

标记顺序树与有界标记脊历史彼此唯一确定，只要保留运算标号、常量、操作数槽与初始标记。该定义
包含混合手性并把标记作为数据的一部分；早期草稿把某些纯手性情形称为线状表达式。
