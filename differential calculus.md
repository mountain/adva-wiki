# Differential calculus

The **differential calculus** of the arithmetic [[contact structure]] is the
horizontal calculus built on the [[expression differential]] $\delta$: where the
ordinary [[de Rham differential]] $d$ differentiates in all directions of the
carrier $(u,v,a)$, $\delta$ keeps only the horizontal part determined by the
propagation law, removing the vertical component —

$$\delta F=dF-(\partial_aF)\alpha$$

for a scalar field $F$.  Thus $\delta$ is a [[horizontal covariant differential]],
not a second copy of the de Rham operator; its restriction to scalar fields is
the [[scalar horizontal differential]], and the whole is the
[[horizontal differential calculus]] attached to the [[contact model]].

The calculus is curvature-sensitive and generally not nilpotent: $\delta^2\ne0$,
and it does not form an ordinary graded differential complex.  It differentiates
only along the two horizontal arithmetic directions, and its curvature density
is the constant $\mu\lambda$.

# 微分演算

算术接触结构的**微分演算**是建立在表达式微分 $\delta$ 上的水平演算：普通 de Rham 微分 $d$
在载体 $(u,v,a)$ 的所有方向求导，而 $\delta$ 只保留由传播律决定的水平部分，去掉垂直分量——

$$\delta F=dF-(\partial_aF)\alpha$$

对标量场 $F$。故 $\delta$ 是水平共变微分，而非 de Rham 算子的第二个副本；它在标量场上的限制
是标量水平微分，整体是附着于接触模型的水平微分演算。

该演算是曲率敏感的，一般非幂零：$\delta^2\ne0$，不构成普通分次微分复形。它只沿两个水平算术
方向求导，其曲率密度是常数 $\mu\lambda$。
