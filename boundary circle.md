# Boundary circle

The **boundary circle** is the ideal boundary of a [[hyperbolic model]] — the unit
circle in the [[disc model]], or the line $y=0$ in the upper half-plane model.  It
lies at infinite distance in the [[hyperbolic metric]], and the projective
trichotomy is read off its fixed points: a hyperbolic motion has two fixed
points on the boundary circle, a parabolic motion one, and an elliptic motion
none.

The boundary circle is the conformal/ideal boundary to which the compactification
attaches the cusps; the [[hyperbolic length]] of a hyperbolic motion is its
translation along the [[geodesic]] joining its two boundary fixed points.

# 边界圆

**边界圆**是双曲模型的理想边界——圆盘模型中的单位圆，或上半平面模型中的直线 $y=0$。它在双曲
度量中位于无穷远处，射影三分法由其不动点读出：双曲运动在边界圆上有两个不动点，抛物一个，椭圆
无。

边界圆是紧化附着尖点所依赖的共形/理想边界；双曲运动的双曲长度是其沿连接两个边界不动点的测地线
的平移。
