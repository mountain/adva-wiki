# Finite carrier

A **finite carrier** ([[finite-type carrier]]) is a connected [[oriented surface]]
$M$ of finite topological type on which the [[arithmetic expression]] space data
are carried: it is diffeomorphic to $\overline M\setminus S$, where
$\overline M$ is a closed oriented surface of genus $\gamma$ and $S$ is a
finite set of $k$ points.  Finiteness means finite genus and finitely many
[[puncture|punctures]].  It is a [[punctured AES]] carrier when a metric and
[[assignment]] $(g,a;\mu,\lambda)$ satisfy the punctured AES definition, with
essentiality at each puncture; the oriented conformal structure is the one
determined by $g$.

The carrier is kept separate from the data it carries.  Its conformal point
compactification adds one point at each end and extends the conformal
structure only — it asserts nothing about extending $g$ or $a$ — and it exists
precisely when every end has a neighbourhood conformally equivalent to a
punctured disc.  For $2\gamma-2+k>0$ the carrier admits a [[pants decomposition]]
into three-ended blocks.  A compactifiable punctured AES exists for every
positive [[puncture count]] $k\ge1$.

# 有限载体

**有限载体**（有限型载体）是承载算术表达式空间数据的、具有有限拓扑型的连通定向曲面：
它微分同胚于 $\overline M\setminus S$，其中 $\overline M$ 是亏格 $\gamma$ 的闭定向曲面，
$S$ 是 $k$ 个点的有限集。有限性指亏格有限且穿孔数有限。当度量与赋值
$(g,a;\mu,\lambda)$ 满足穿孔 AES 定义（在每个穿孔处本质），它就是穿孔 AES 载体；
定向共形结构由 $g$ 决定。

载体与其所承载的数据是分开的。它的共形点紧化在每个端加上一个点，仅延伸共形结构——
不涉及 $g$ 或 $a$ 的延伸——当且仅当每个端有邻域共形等价于穿孔圆盘时存在。当
$2\gamma-2+k>0$ 时载体有裤分解，分为三端块。对每个正穿孔数 $k\ge1$，都存在可紧化的
穿孔 AES。
