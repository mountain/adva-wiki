# Ordered history

An **ordered history** is a [[process history]] carrying its explicit
[[chronological order]] — the literal sequence of steps, before any
commutativization or endpoint.  It retains what a commutative completion
forgets: the [[accumulative commutative space]] keeps only the [[total charge]], and
the [[charge endpoint]] keeps only the terminal value, while the ordered history
keeps the order itself.

Task-relevant ordered history cannot in general be reconstructed from the
commutative completion, so forgetting the order is a genuine loss; this is the
noncommutative necessity of keeping the process history ordered.

# 有序历史

**有序历史**是携带其显式时间顺序的过程历史——各步的字面序列，在任何交换化或端点之前。它保留交换
完成所遗忘者：累积交换空间只保留总荷，荷端点只保留终值，而有序历史保留次序本身。

任务相关的有序历史一般不能从交换完成重建，故遗忘次序是真实损失；这正是保留有序过程历史的非交换
必要性。
