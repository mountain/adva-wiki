# Braid transport

**Braid transport** is the reversible, order-preserving movement of complete
typed ports across a fixed boundary — the braid (Artin) action on the boundary
ports — as distinct from the non-invertible updates that perform computation.
It records an exact [[braid history]] with bijective endpoint routing.

[[Transport]] is the generic word; braid is its group-view reading, and conjugacy
arises only under that additional view.  Braid coherence is a checked equality
between two [[transport history|transport histories]].  In the checked gate–braid composition, the
braid layer is a [[transport sidecar]], not yet a computationally coupled
substrate: crossings move complete colored ports while the gate reads only
their colors and values, so every pure braid lies in a contextual kernel of the
finite computation.

# 辫传输

**辫传输**是完整类型端口跨固定边界的可逆、保序移动——边界端口上的辫（Artin）作用——区别于执行
计算的不可逆更新。它记录精确的辫历史，端点路由是双射的。

传输是通用词；辫是它的群观点解读，而共轭只在该额外观点下出现。辫相干性是两条传输历史之间的
经检验等式。在经检验的闸-辫复合中，辫层是传输侧车，而非已计算耦合的基底：跨越移动完整的
着色端口，而闸只读其颜色与值，故每个纯辫都落在有限计算的上下文核中。
