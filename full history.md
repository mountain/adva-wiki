# Full history

The **full history** of a process is its complete, [[literal history]] — the finest
record that keeps every distinction of the process, from which every quotient
forgets.  Its [[history unfolding]] is the maximal unfold: a task-sufficient
lift lies strictly between the full history and the task quotient.

A visible graph's [[universal cover]] is not automatically the full history
unfolding, and an analytic or [[topological cover]] is not the full history either;
a literal history records the raw word, while the full history retains every
process distinction that a quotient, an observer, or a cover may collapse.  The
[[retained history]] is the part of the full history that a [[transport]] keeps.

# 全历史

过程的**全历史**是其完整、字面的历史——保留过程每个区分的最细记录，任何商都从它遗忘。其历史
展开是极大展开：任务充分提升严格位于全历史与任务商之间。

可见图的万有覆盖不自动是全历史展开，解析或拓扑覆盖也不是全历史；字面历史记录原始词，而全历史
保留商、观察者或覆盖可能坍缩的每个过程区分。保留历史是传输所保留的全历史的那部分。
