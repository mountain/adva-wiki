# Riemannian surface

A **Riemannian surface** $(M,g)$ is a two-dimensional smooth manifold carrying a
[[riemannian metric]] $g$; with an orientation it is an [[oriented riemannian surface]],
the setting of a [[regular arithmetic expression space]].  It is a metric object, and
must be distinguished from a [[riemann surface]], which is a one-dimensional
complex manifold.

A regular arithmetic expression space is an oriented Riemannian surface
together with a smooth [[assignment]] $a$ satisfying the [[eikonal]] identity, on which
the [[canonical arithmetic frame]] is the unique positively oriented orthonormal
frame with $X_u a=\mu$ and $X_v a=\lambda a$.

# 黎曼曲面

**黎曼曲面** $(M,g)$ 是携带黎曼度量 $g$ 的二维光滑流形；带上定向即为定向黎曼曲面，是正则算术
表达式空间的设定。它是度量对象，必须区别于一维复流形的黎曼面。

正则算术表达式空间是定向黎曼曲面连同满足程函恒等式的光滑赋值 $a$，其上规范算术标架是满足
$X_u a=\mu$、$X_v a=\lambda a$ 的唯一正定向正交标架。
