# Spectral carrier

A **spectral carrier** is the proposed carrier that retains an operator together
with its spectral structure, so that its spectrum is carried with the structure
that produces it.  For the inversion $J$, the characteristic polynomial is
$\chi_J(\lambda)=(\lambda^2+1)^3$, and the matrix alone is only a coordinate
shadow: the proposed spectral carrier is the [[six state carrier]] $W$ together
with its three aspect-opposite-relation planes and the global orientation
action, which record why those planes are paired.

Any proposed spectral carrier must retain the operator, not merely its matrix
coordinates; eigenvalues and [[holonomy]] are not yet claimed as part of a stable
spectral carrier.

# 谱载体

**谱载体**是保留算子及其谱结构、使谱连同产生它的结构一起被携带的提议载体。对反演 $J$，特征多项
式为 $\chi_J(\lambda)=(\lambda^2+1)^3$，矩阵单独只是坐标影子：提议的谱载体是六态载体 $W$ 连同其
三个 aspect-opposite-relation 平面与全局定向作用，它们记录这些平面为何成对。

任何提议谱载体必须保留算子本身而非仅其矩阵坐标；特征值与和乐尚未作为稳定谱载体的一部分被
声明。
