# Observer transport

**Observer transport** is the controlled movement of an observation from one
observer chart to another.  A process $P$ induces a geometry $\mathcal G_O(P)$
for each observer $O$; observer transport asks when information seen in one
observer has a canonical or controlled transfer to another,
$\mathcal G_{O_1}(P)\leftrightarrow\mathcal G_{O_2}(P)$, rather than postulating
a connection first.

Possible [[transport|transports]] are refinement maps between quotients, local-to-global
reconstruction, comparison of local invariants, continuation along a family of
observers, and the obstruction or [[curvature]] recorded when transports around a
loop fail to close.  On the Adva side, transport is a homogeneous crossing of an
observer structure — contravariant with respect to the data it observes — and it
must carry an explicit preservation relation, not a naming analogy.

An observer transport must record its preservation relation: a change of
coordinates is conjugation together with the transported predicate, and a
one-sided map without that transport is not accepted.

# 观察者传输

**观察者传输**是把一次观察从一个观察者图受控地移到另一个观察者图。过程 $P$ 对每个观察者
$O$ 诱导一个几何 $\mathcal G_O(P)$；观察者传输问的是，一个观察者所见的信息何时能典范或受控地
转移到另一个，$\mathcal G_{O_1}(P)\leftrightarrow\mathcal G_{O_2}(P)$，而不是先假定一个联络。

可能的传输包括商之间的细化映射、局部到全局的重构、局部不变量比较、沿观察者族的延拓，以及
绕环路传输不闭合时的阻碍或曲率。在 Adva 一侧，传输是观察者结构的同质穿越——关于它所观察的
数据是反变的——并且必须携带显式的保持关系，而非命名类比。

观察者传输必须记录其保持关系：坐标变换是共轭加上被传输的谓词，而没有该传输的单侧映射不被
接受。
