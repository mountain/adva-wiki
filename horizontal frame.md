# Horizontal frame

The **horizontal frame** $(e_u,e_v)$ is the preferred [[orthonormal frame]] of the
horizontal distribution of the [[contact model]], taken $h$-orthonormal and
positively oriented for the declared [[horizontal metric]] $h=du^2+dv^2$.  It is
the frame on which the [[curvature form]] is evaluated: $\omega(e_u,e_v)=\mu\lambda$.

It is the horizontal counterpart of the orthonormal frame of the surface; the
[[curvature scalar]] $\mu\lambda$ is the value of $\omega$ on this frame, and its
residual freedom is the rescaling of $h$.

# 水平标架

**水平标架** $(e_u,e_v)$ 是接触模型水平分布的优选正交标架，对声明的水平度量 $h=du^2+dv^2$ 取
$h$-正交且正定向。它是曲率形式所取值其上的标架：$\omega(e_u,e_v)=\mu\lambda$。

它是曲面正交标架的水平对应；曲率标量 $\mu\lambda$ 是 $\omega$ 在此标架上的值，其残差自由是 $h$
的重标度。
