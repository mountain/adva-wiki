# Scope path

A **scope path** is the ordered path through the nested scopes of a graft or
frame — the position of a term in the forward scope organization.  Swapping the
two output terms changes the graft scope path, and a `GraftFrame` records the
scope path together with the caller and the callee.

The scope path is the forward organization, related to but distinct from the
[[scope cut]] incidence and the [[causal cut|causal cuts]]; its relationship to the
causal cuts is not a bijection.

# 作用域路径

**作用域路径**是 graft 或框架嵌套作用域的路径——项在前向作用域组织中的位置。交换两个输出项改变
graft 作用域路径，`GraftFrame` 记录作用域路径连同调用者与被调用者。

作用域路径是前向组织，与作用域切割关联及因果切割相关但不同；它与因果切割的关系不是双射。
