# Iota frame

An **iota frame** is an [[interpretation frame]] that carries iota, $i$, and $e$
together.  A [[frame binds]] one retained process to the representations and
observations in which a question is asked; in the iota frame the roles are kept
distinct: iota is the sole primitive source combinator (application, apertures,
and [[process history]] remain explicit), $i$ is the complex structure $J$ with
$J^2=-I$ (a representation structure, not a source combinator or a native
scalar), and $e$ is the exponential coefficient rule $A^k/k!$ (a process
observation, distinct from a pointwise exponential).

The frame further separates explicit construction, space, and time policies
($\{\}$, $[]$, $()$); the declared operator, metric, and observer ($H,G,O$); a
dependency-respecting event clock; and the resource, evidence, and residual
scope of a judgment.  The iota frame is an implemented finite research profile:
it registers no native keyword, operation, or stable semantic type, and general
frame formation remains open.

# iota 框架

**iota 框架**是把 iota、$i$ 与 $e$ 一并承载的解释框架。框架把一个被保留的过程绑定到提问所
用的表示与观察上；iota 框架中这些角色保持区分：iota 是唯一的原始源组合子（应用、孔径与过程
历史保持显式），$i$ 是满足 $J^2=-I$ 的复结构 $J$（表示结构，而非源组合子或原生标量），$e$ 是
指数系数规则 $A^k/k!$（过程观察，区别于逐点指数）。

该框架进一步区分显式的构造、空间与时间策略（$\{\}$、$[]$、$()$）；声明的算子、度量与观察者
（$H,G,O$）；尊重依赖的事件钟；以及判断的资源、证据与残差范围。iota 框架是已实现的有限研究
档案：它不注册原生关键字、运算或稳定语义类型，一般框架构造仍未解决。
