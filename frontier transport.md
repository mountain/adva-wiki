# Frontier transport

**Frontier transport** is the elementary [[transport]] of a [[cut frontier]] by an
enabled event: advancing the [[causal cut]] $U\mapsto U\cup\{e\}$ moves one
operation through the cut, realized as expression substitution plus the
transport of the cut frontier.  Enabled events transport those frontiers, and
independent checked frontier transports generate a local interchange.

Frontier transport is distinct from [[boundary transport]] and from a stable
observer pullback; it was reconstructed only in Python and its native
reconstruction is part of the program-process core.

# 前沿传输

**前沿传输**是切割前沿被可用事件的基本传输：推进因果切割 $U\mapsto U\cup\{e\}$ 使一个运算穿过
切割，实现为表达式代入加切割前沿的传输。可用事件传输那些前沿，独立的经检验前沿传输生成局部
互换。

前沿传输区别于边界传输与稳定观察者拉回；它仅在 Python 中重建，其原生重建是程序-过程核心的
一部分。
