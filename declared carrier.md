# Declared carrier

A **declared carrier** is a carrier whose directions are explicitly declared: a
finite-dimensional module over a declared set of cut directions, read from one
checked diagram.  The directions are the simultaneously enabled unary events on
pairwise disjoint source-bearing frontier wires; they are an index set, not an
operation, and they are not inferred from numeric values.

The checker works on the declared signed basis of the carrier.  The [[six state carrier]]
of the causal-cut calibration is one finite instance, and the [[exact carrier]] is
the identity-rich declared carrier that retains full decoration.

# 声明载体

**声明载体**是其方向被显式声明的载体：声明切割方向集上的有限维模，从一张经检验图读出。方向是
两两不交源承载前沿线上同时可用的单元事件；它们是指标集，而非运算，也不从数值推断。

检查器在载体的声明符号基上工作。因果切割校准的六态载体是一个有限实例，精确载体则是保留完整
装饰的身份丰富声明载体。
