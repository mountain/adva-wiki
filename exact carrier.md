# Exact carrier

An **exact carrier** is the [[finite carrier]] that retains the exact, fully
decorated data — sources, occurrences, and incidence — before any quotient or
observer descent collapses it.  A declared surjection $q:C\to Q$ is the
quotient, and a proposition region is $q$-saturated when it is a union of whole
fibres; such a region descends to $Q$ uniquely, while a non-saturated region is
reported as such rather than silently merged.

The exact carrier must retain the decorated members of each fibre, and it is the
object on which exact states are defined and on which composition acts; it is
paired with the [[observable quotient]] and the [[quotient descent]] it admits.
It is one of the certified research types — exact carrier, relation, and
quotient — and is not by itself a quotient or an identity merge.

# 精确载体

**精确载体**是保留精确、完整装饰数据（源、出现与关联）的有限载体，在任何商或观察者下降坍缩它
之前。声明的满射 $q:C\to Q$ 是商，命题区域当它是整条纤维的并时为 $q$-饱和；这样的区域唯一地
下降到 $Q$，而非饱和区域被如实报告，而非悄悄合并。

精确载体必须保留每条纤维的装饰成员，它是定义精确状态、作用复合的对象；它与它容许的可观测量商
与商下降相配。它是经认证的研究类型之一——精确载体、关系与商——本身既非商也非等同合并。
