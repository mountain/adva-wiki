# Composition order

The **composition order** is the [[declared order]] in which operations are
composed, read in a fixed [[convention]] (e.g. right-to-left).  For noncommutative
operations the composition order matters: composing $J_{\mathcal K\mathcal X}$
before $J_{\mathcal Xt}$ differs from the reverse, and a law is checked for
insensitivity to the reversed composition order.

The composition order is the [[chronological order]] of the operations; it is
distinct from the [[evaluation order]] and from the [[matrix multiplication]]
convention, and a declared composition order is part of the volume-word data.

# 复合序

**复合序**是运算被复合的声明次序，以固定约定（如从右到左）读出。对非交换运算，复合序重要：先
复合 $J_{\mathcal K\mathcal X}$ 再 $J_{\mathcal Xt}$ 与相反不同，且律被检验为对反向复合序不敏感。

复合序是运算的时间顺序；它区别于求值序与矩阵乘法约定，声明的复合序是体积词数据的一部分。
