# Compositional rank

The **compositional rank** is the [[vertical rank]] in the objectification tower: a
stable lower-rank process objectifies into a new primitive, which then generates
a [[higher rank]] free composition language whose legal words must lower
compositionally back to the lower-rank semantics.  Objectification can open new
compositional ranks, and the [[process rank]] and the [[arithmetic rank]] are its
two concrete readings.

A [[rank lowering]] is the requirement that higher-rank compositions admit a
coherent interpretation into lower-rank semantics; this is the
[[compositional rank lowering]], without which a proposed primitive is not an
objectified one.

# 复合秩

**复合秩**是对象化塔中的纵向秩：稳定的低秩过程对象化为新原始，随后生成更高秩的自由复合语言，
其合法词必须复合地下降到低秩语义。对象化可开辟新的复合秩，过程秩与算术秩是其两个具体读数。

秩下降是更高秩复合须容许到低秩语义的一致解释的要求；这即复合秩下降，没有它提议原始就不是已
对象化原始。
