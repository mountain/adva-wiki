# Addition multiplication grid

The **addition multiplication grid** is the grid in $E_0$ generated by addition
and multiplication acting as motions: addition is the [[assignment]] translation
$\mathsf X_s(x,y)=(x-sy,y)$ (a horizontal move of the assignment), and
multiplication is the assignment scaling $\mathsf Y_k(x,y)=(x,y/k)$ (a vertical
move).  It is the [[arithmetic grid]] of $E_0$, also called the [[e0 grid]] and
the [[upper half plane grid]].

The grid is assignment-compatible but not generally isometric: its horizontal
and vertical moves record the two arithmetic directions, and the noncommutativity
of addition and multiplication is read from the grid.

# 加法乘法网格

**加法乘法网格**是 $E_0$ 中由加法与乘法作为运动生成的网格：加法是赋值平移
$\mathsf X_s(x,y)=(x-sy,y)$（赋值的水平移动），乘法是赋值伸缩 $\mathsf Y_k(x,y)=(x,y/k)$（竖直
移动）。它是 $E_0$ 的算术网格，也称 E0 网格与上半平面网格。

网格赋值相容但一般非等距：其水平与竖直移动记录两个算术方向，加法与乘法的非交换性从网格读出。
