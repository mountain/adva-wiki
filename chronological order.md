# Chronological order

The **chronological order** is the [[temporal order]] in which the steps of a history
are applied: the index of a [[marked spinal history]] is chronological, so the
word $\gamma=(g_1,\ldots,g_n)$ records the steps in the order they occur.  It is
the temporal order of the operations, and it is the order of the
[[composition order]] of the [[induced motion|induced motions]].

The chronological order is distinct from the [[dependency order]], which orders
operands by which feeds which, and from the [[evaluation order]]; two histories may
share a dependency order while differing in chronological order.

# 时间先后序

**时间先后序**是历史各步被应用的时间顺序：标记脊历史的索引是时间先后的，故词
$\gamma=(g_1,\ldots,g_n)$ 按步发生的次序记录各步。它是运算的时间序，也是诱导运动的复合序的次序。

时间先后序区别于按谁供给谁排序的依赖序，也区别于求值序；两条历史可共享依赖序而在时间先后序上
不同。
