# History language

The **history language** is the free monoid of histories — the set of all finite
words over the alphabet of elementary steps (or elementary one-hole contexts),
before evaluation.  In the add–scale setting it is the free monoid generated by
the two families of one-step histories, the additive steps and the
multiplicative steps.

The [[accumulative commutative space]] is the commutativization of the history
language: imposing that the two families commute.  The history language is the
syntactic layer to which the [[history group]] and the [[marked spinal history]]
are attached; it retains every word, while evaluation and charge forget
structure.

# 历史语言

**历史语言**是历史的自由幺半群——初等步（或初等单孔语境）字母表上所有有限词的集合，在求值之前。
在加减情形，它是由两族单步历史（加法步与乘法步）生成的自由幺半群。

累积交换空间是历史语言的交换化：强加两族可交换。历史语言是历史群与标记脊历史所依附的句法层；
它保留每个词，而求值与荷遗忘结构。
