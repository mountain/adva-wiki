# Total charge

The **total charge** of an add–scale history $\gamma$ is the pair
$(A_\gamma,M_\gamma)$ of its [[total additive charge]] and total multiplicative
charge — the sum of all charge increments, independent of their order.  It is
the image of $\gamma$ under the [[charge map]] $c:G\to\mathbb R^2$, the
abelianization.

The total charge is the [[charge endpoint]] of the [[charge path]] $C_\gamma$:
two histories of the same total charge have the same endpoint, and only the
multiplicative component descends to the [[motion group]].

# 总荷

加减历史 $\gamma$ 的**总荷**是其总加法荷与总乘法荷对 $(A_\gamma,M_\gamma)$——所有荷增量之和，与
其次序无关。它是 $\gamma$ 在荷映射 $c:G\to\mathbb R^2$（阿贝尔化）下的像。

总荷即荷路径 $C_\gamma$ 的荷端点：总荷相同的两条历史有相同端点，只有乘法分量下降到运动群。
