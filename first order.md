# First order

**First order** denotes the level-one algebraic image of a process: the pair
$(F,DF)$ — the observable together with its first differential — giving the
[[first order observable]] and its [[first order expansion]].  It is the first
jet, as opposed to higher-order (second-harmonic and beyond) data.

The first-order coefficient-jet complexity is $2$ in fixed, noncanonical
controls and $0$ on the [[canonical lift]], so canonicalization already removes the
first-order residual; a genuine obstruction is a [[second order obstruction]], not
a first-order one.

# 一阶

**一阶**表示过程的层级一代数像：对 $(F,DF)$——可观测量连同其第一微分——给出第一阶可观测量及其
第一阶展开。它是第一射流，与高阶（二次谐波及更高）数据相对。

一阶系数射流复杂度在固定非规范控制下为 $2$、在规范提升上为 $0$，故规范化已移除一阶残差；真正
的阻碍是二阶阻碍，而非一阶。
