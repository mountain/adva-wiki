# Predicate transport

**Predicate transport** is the [[backward transport]] of predicates and observations
— the inverse-image pullback $f^*(P)=f^{-1}(P)$, dual to the forward
[[state transport]].  It is the backward logical observation
$f^{-1}:\Omega(Y)\to\Omega(X)$: the truth of a transported predicate is read on
the preimage.

Forward actions dualize to backward predicate transformers, so a chronological
history becomes reverse predicate transport exactly; the two sides are the
[[state observer duality]].  It is the contravariant side of backward transport.

# 谓词传输

**谓词传输**是谓词与观察的后向传输——逆像拉回 $f^*(P)=f^{-1}(P)$，与前向状态传输对偶。它是后向
逻辑观察 $f^{-1}:\Omega(Y)\to\Omega(X)$：所传输谓词的真值在原像上读出。

前向作用对偶化为后向谓词变换器，故时间顺序历史恰成为反向谓词传输；两侧即状态-观察者对偶。它
是后向传输的反变一侧。
