# Oriented lift

An **oriented lift** is the orientation-preserving lift of a projective
transformation to an oriented (matrix) representative — a refinement of the
[[linear lift]] that records the orientation sign.  A single projective
time–space exchange has two oriented lifts, which differ by the central sign
$-I$ and coincide projectively.

The orientation-preserving lift has order four: for the negative reciprocal
$J:z\mapsto-1/z$ one has $J^2=-I$ in the oriented lift and projective square
$1$.  The two oriented lifts are distinguished by the forward/reverse surgery
sign, and are not distinguished by a projective readout alone.

# 定向提升

**定向提升**是射影变换到定向（矩阵）代表的保定向提升——记录定向符号的线性提升的细化。单个射影
时空交换有两个定向提升，相差中心符号 $-I$ 且射影地重合。

保定向提升有四阶：对负倒数 $J:z\mapsto-1/z$，在定向提升中 $J^2=-I$、射影平方为 $1$。两个定向
提升由前向/反向手术符号区分，仅凭射影读数无法区分。
