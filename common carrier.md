# Common carrier

A **common carrier** is a carrier shared by several mechanisms or processes —
the common finite [[process carrier]] on which they act.  The common carrier of
causality and cuts is promoted into the semantic core, and angle extraction
depends only on the common [[exact carrier]]; the three cusp channels act on a
common rank-two carrier.

A common carrier may fail to exist: the AEG left and [[right expansion|right expansions]] have no
common carrier on which both act, and no ordinary spectrum is read before a
common carrier, a round trip, and an adjoint pair are supplied.  It is distinct
from a [[declared carrier]] and a [[state carrier]], which need not be shared.

# 公共载体

**公共载体**是多个机制或过程共享的载体——它们作用其上的公共有限过程载体。因果与切割的公共载体
被提升进语义核心，角度提取只依赖公共精确载体；三个尖点通道作用在公共二阶载体上。

公共载体可能不存在：AEG 左右展开没有二者都作用其上的公共载体，且在给出公共载体、往返与伴随对
之前不读出普通谱。它区别于声明载体与状态载体，后二者不必被共享。
