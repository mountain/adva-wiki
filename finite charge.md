# Finite charge

A **finite charge** is the finite charge data on which a construction or square
is read — for example a construction-side charge $q\in\mathbb Z^2$ — as opposed
to the [[construction history]] that still grows.  A square may commute on its
finite charge while the lifted construction history does not, and the same
finite charge can have multiple lifted constructions.

The finite charge is coarser than the construction history and finer than a bare
value; it is the finite data analogous to the [[total charge]] and the [[acs charge]]
of the arithmetic sector, read at the [[charge endpoint]].

# 有限荷

**有限荷**是构造或方块所读出的有限荷数据——例如构造侧荷 $q\in\mathbb Z^2$——与仍增长的构造历史
相对。方块可在其有限荷上交换而提升构造历史不交换，同一有限荷可有多个提升构造。

有限荷比构造历史粗、比裸值细；它是算术扇形总荷与 ACS 荷的有限数据类比，在荷端点读出。
