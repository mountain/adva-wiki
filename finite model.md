# Finite model

A **finite model** is an explicitly finite instance of a structure — a finite
state set, a finite group, a finite vocabulary, a finite cellulation, or a
finite enumeration — on which a statement is asserted or checked exactly,
usually exhaustively, rather than for an arbitrary or infinite object.  The
term is methodological: the theory itself lives over [[smooth surface|smooth surfaces]], infinite
[[history group|history groups]], and general fields, and a finite model replaces one of these
with a stipulated finite object.

Two uses recur in the AEG series.  First, a finite model can realize an
abstract or infinite structure exactly: an abstract [[torsor]] may have an
exact finite model, and a theorem about a closed finite quotient is read as
the finite model of a general proposition.  Second, a finite model can be the
chosen arena of a computation.  In Paper 0 the six holed-[[AES]] witnesses are
"about chosen finite models": a chosen gluing family, a chosen frame, a chosen
vocabulary, a chosen stopping rule; changing the model changes the count.

The status discipline is the point.  A result established in a finite model is
a computationally verified example, valid for that stated model alone; it does
not by itself yield a general theorem or obstruction.  In particular no
finite-model witness shows that [[puncture|punctures]] are unavoidable in an [[arithmetic expression]]
space.

# 有限模型

**有限模型**是某个结构的显式有限实例——有限状态集、有限群、有限词汇、有限胞腔划分或
有限枚举——在其上对某个陈述作精确的（通常是穷尽的）断言或检验，而非对任意或无穷对象。
该词是方法性的：理论本身生活在光滑曲面、无穷历史群与一般域之上，而有限模型以某个约定的
有限对象取代其中之一。

AEG 系列中它有两种反复出现的用法。其一，有限模型可精确实现某个抽象或无穷结构：一个抽象
挠子可有精确有限模型，而关于闭有限商的一个定理被读作某个一般命题的有限模型。其二，有限
模型可以是某次计算的选定竞技场。Paper 0 中六个穿孔 AES 见证都是"关于选定的有限模型"：
选定的粘合族、选定的框架、选定的词汇、选定的停机规则；更换模型即更换计数。

关键在状态纪律。在有限模型中建立的结果是计算验证示例，仅对该指定模型成立；它本身不给出
一般定理或阻碍。特别地，没有任何有限模型见证表明穿孔在算术表达式空间中不可避免。
