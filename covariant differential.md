# Covariant differential

A **covariant differential** is the differential operator induced by a
connection: it differentiates along the horizontal directions of the
connection, and it is curvature-sensitive — generally not nilpotent, so it does
not form an ordinary graded differential complex.

In the arithmetic [[contact structure]] the covariant differential is the
[[horizontal covariant differential]] $\delta$, whose restriction to scalar
fields is the [[scalar horizontal differential]]; it keeps only the horizontal
part of the [[de Rham differential]].  It is part of the [[differential calculus]]
attached to the [[contact model]], and $\delta^2\ne0$ in general.

# 共变微分

**共变微分**是由联络诱导的微分算子：沿联络的水平方向求导，且是曲率敏感的——一般非幂零，故不
构成普通分次微分复形。

在算术接触结构中，共变微分即水平共变微分 $\delta$，其在标量场上的限制是标量水平微分；它只保留
de Rham 微分的水平部分。它是附着于接触模型的微分演算的一部分，一般 $\delta^2\ne0$。
