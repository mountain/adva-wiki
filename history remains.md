# History remains

**History remains** is the history that survives an operation — the question of
what history is still present after a circuit, a quotient, or a [[transport]].  It
may remain as a fibre, or as a separate signed transport layer; the raw process
history remains a separate requirement.

The history that remains is the [[residual history]] carried by a [[process residue]],
and it is the part of the [[retained history]] that is still attached after the
operation.

# 历史留存

**历史留存**是运算后存活的历史——一轮回路、商或传输之后仍存在什么历史的问题。它可作为纤维留存，
或作为独立的符号传输层留存；原始过程历史留存为独立要求。

留存的历史是由过程残差携带的残差历史，是运算后仍附着的保留历史的那部分。
